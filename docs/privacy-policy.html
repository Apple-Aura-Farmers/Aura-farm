Terraloka Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Terraloka helps field helpers map farm plots and helps microscope operators count soil organisms in a sample. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Terraloka iOS app only.

What we collect

Everything below is linked to your account. None of it is used for tracking, advertising or advertising measurement, and none of it is sold or shared with data brokers.

DataWhat it isWhy
Name The name on your account, and the farmer name recorded against a farm. So a sample can be filed against the person and land it came from.
Email address Used to create and sign in to an account. Authentication. Not used for marketing.
Precise location Latitude and longitude of plot boundaries you draw, including points taken from your device's GPS when you mark a corner where you are standing. To record where a plot is and show it on the map.
User ID An account identifier created when you sign in. To keep your records separate from other people's and enforce access rules.
Other user content Soil measurements you enter (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, organic carbon, fertiliser), plot outlines, and the results of a microscope sweep. The core function of the app.
Photos A small cropped picture of each soil organism a test counted, sent with the test when you tap Upload or Publish result. So the count can be checked against what was seen.

All of it is collected for app functionality only.

What stays on your device

The camera looks through the microscope to check image quality and record a sample. Photos and videos you pick from your library are analysed to count organisms. Both are processed on your device.

Only the small cropped organism pictures described above are uploaded, and only when you send a test. The full microscope pictures, any video recorded during a test and the photos and videos you pick from your library are not uploaded, and the slide picture is removed from a report before it is sent.

Your work is saved on your device first, so the app keeps working with no signal. It is sent to our records when a connection is available.

Information about other people

If you are a field helper, the farms you map include a farmer's name and the location of their land. Please only record this with that person's knowledge. You are responsible for telling them their details are held in Terraloka, and you can ask us to remove them at any time using the contact address below.

Where it goes

Your data is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase, which processes it on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. Access is restricted by row-level security rules, so an account can only reach the records its role permits.

We do not use any advertising networks, analytics services, attribution SDKs or crash reporting services. No third party receives your data for their own use.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while your account is active, so that soil tests can be compared across seasons. Deleting a farm or a plot in the app removes its records. Organism pictures already sent with a test are stored separately and are not erased by that; write to us and we will delete them. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove your account and the records created under it, including those pictures.

Your choices

  • Location. You can refuse or withdraw location access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. The app still works; you draw plot corners by tapping the map instead.
  • Camera and photos. You can refuse or withdraw access in Settings. Sample recording will not work without the camera.
  • Access, correction and deletion. Write to us and we will give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it.
  • Sign out at any time from the Account screen.

If you are in the UK, EU or another region with similar laws, you also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. Our lawful basis is the performance of our agreement with you and our legitimate interest in recording soil tests accurately.

Children

Terraloka is a tool for agricultural work and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, say so in the app.
